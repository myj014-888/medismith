MediSmith — asset drop-in guide
================================

LOGO
  assets/logo.png        <- the gold cross + "medismith" lockup from the brand board.
                            Transparent PNG or SVG, ~1200px wide. When present it
                            automatically replaces the built-in fallback wordmark.
  assets/favicon.png     <- 512x512 square, gold cross on dark.
